02 / 08
The Problem

Service clubs run
on scattered tools.

📋
Manual hour tracking
Spreadsheets, paper logs, and DM chains — no single source of truth for volunteer hours.
📅
Event chaos
Sign-ups spread across forms, email threads, and group chats that nobody can find later.
🌍
Invisible impact
Hours are approved (or lost) with no record of where in the community the work happened.
✅
Approval bottlenecks
Advisors scramble to verify submissions with no audit trail and no easy bulk tools.
03 / 08
The Solution

Everything a club
needs in one
calm place.

VolunteerClub gives student service clubs a single platform to run their entire operation — from member onboarding to impact visualization.

📅
Event management
Create events, manage attendance, QR check-in, and hour auto-grant
⏱
Hour tracking & approval
Submit, review, approve, reject, and export hours with full audit trail
🗺️
Territory map
Interactive world map shows per-location impact and expanding club territories
🤝
Partner affiliations
Connect with nonprofits, food banks, and local businesses for service requests
04 / 08
A small rhythm that works

Start with the next
good thing.

01
Bring your people together
Create a club, invite members, and give the right people a say in approvals. Private or public — your choice.
02
Show up and log it
Run an event or add hours after the fact. Location, proof images, and notes keep the story intact.
03
Make the impact visible
Approved hours build a real record — by person, event, and place — and expand your club's territory on the map.
05 / 08
Feature depth

Built for how clubs
actually operate.

📊
Admin dashboard
Approve/reject submissions, grant hours directly, manage members, archive school years.
📷
Proof uploads
Volunteers attach image proof; admins review it inline before approving hours.
📱
QR check-in / out
Generate event QR codes; volunteers scan in and out, hours calculated automatically.
🏆
Leaderboards
Per-club member boards plus global club rankings by approved hours contributed.
📁
Logs & history
Named tracking periods (e.g. "Fall Semester") with archive, export, and per-student history.
🌐
Super-club federation
Sub-clubs roll up hours to a parent super-club with affiliation approval and cross-club leaderboards.

07 / 08
Under the hood

Modern stack,
zero friction.

⚛️
React 18 + TypeScript
Vite-powered frontend with TanStack Query for server state, Zod for validation, and shadcn/ui components.
🔥
Firebase
Firestore for real-time data across 9 collections. Firebase Auth with Google OAuth for seamless sign-in.
🚀
Express.js API
Node.js backend with Firebase Admin SDK, session management, and RESTful endpoints for all club operations.
🗺️
MapLibre GL
Interactive map rendering with react-map-gl. Nominatim for geocoding. Custom territory circle rendering.
