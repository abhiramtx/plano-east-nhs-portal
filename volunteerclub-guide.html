02
The big picture

So, what is
VolunteerClub?

VolunteerClub is a platform for student service clubs. It gives every member — and every club admin — one place to manage everything, from joining a club to tracking every volunteer hour.

Think of it as the digital home for your club. Instead of spreadsheets, email chains, and paper logs, everything lives here.

For volunteers

Submit hours, track approvals, join events, and see your impact on the map.

For admins

Review submissions, manage members, run events, and archive school years.

For affiliated clubs

Connect with parent organizations and pool your service hours together.

The core unit

What is a Club?

A Club is the home base for a group of volunteers. Everything in VolunteerClub revolves around clubs — your hour submissions belong to a club, your leaderboard ranking is within a club, and your territory on the map grows for your club.

You can be a member of one club at a time. Inside a club, you're either a Volunteer (regular member) or an Admin (who can approve hours and manage the club).

Public clubs

Anyone can search for and join a public club. No invite required.

Private clubs

You need an invite link or QR code from a club admin to join a private club.

Want to start your own?

On the Club Selection page, click New Club. You'll automatically be its first admin.

📂
Before you submit hours

What is a
"Log Period"?

A Log Period is a named container for your hour submissions — like "Fall Semester 2025" or "Spring Community Service." Your admin creates these to organize submissions by time period or campaign.

When you submit hours, you pick which Log Period they belong to. This helps admins review and report hours by period instead of looking at one giant unorganized list.

Open logs

You can submit hours to any log that's currently open. Your admin controls when logs open and close.

Closed logs

Once a log is closed, you can still view it but can no longer submit to it. Closed logs are read-only.

Hours Required

Each log may have a minimum hours goal (e.g. 25 hrs). It's shown on the log card so you know your target.

🔄
After you submit

What do the
statuses mean?

⏳ Pending

Waiting for review

Your submission has been received. An admin hasn't looked at it yet. This is the normal first state for every submission.

✓ Approved

Hours counted

An admin reviewed and approved your submission. These hours are now added to your total and count toward your log period goal.

✗ Rejected

Not approved

An admin declined this submission. The hours are not counted. If you think this is a mistake, talk to your club admin directly.

📸 Pro tip
Including a proof photo with your submission makes it much easier for admins to approve quickly. No photo can sometimes mean a slower review or a rejection.
12
👥
Inside your club

What's on the
"My Club" page?

The My Club page is your club's social and stats hub. It shows everyone who's in the club, who's leading in hours, and where your club's service work is showing up on the map.

It has four tabs: Members, Leaderboard, Map, and (for admins) Settings.

Members tab

See every member's name, email, role (Admin or Volunteer), and their total approved hours.

Leaderboard tab

A ranked list of club members sorted by approved hours. See where you stand among your peers.

Map tab

An interactive map showing where your club's volunteer work has happened. Circles grow with your hours.

13
🗺️
My Club → Map tab

How the Territory
Map works

Every time you submit hours with a location and they get approved, that location gains a circle on the map. The more hours approved at a place, the bigger that circle grows.

This is your club's "territory" — a visual record of where your service work is happening in the real world. Hover over any circle to see which member volunteered there.

⏳ Circles shrink over time
If a location goes without new approved hours, its circle shrinks slowly. Staying active keeps your territory growing.

Community opportunities

What are
Service Requests?

Service Requests are volunteer opportunities that anyone can post — organizations looking for help, or volunteers organizing an event and looking for participants.

Think of it like a community bulletin board. You can browse what's available, read the details, and sign up to participate. The organizer sets how many hours you'll earn.

You can also post your own service request if you're organizing something and need volunteers to join.

Available tab

Browse all currently open requests. Click "View" for full details, then "Join" to sign up.

My Requests tab

Requests you created. You can manage participants, approve them, and award hours when the event is done.

Joined tab

Requests you've signed up for. Track your status and wait for the organizer to award hours.

The most misunderstood feature

Let's talk about
Affiliates.

This section takes a bit of explaining — so we're giving it the space it deserves. By the end you'll know exactly what affiliations are, why they exist, and how they work.

18
🔗
Affiliates, explained

What does
"affiliated" mean?

Affiliation is a way for clubs to connect in a parent-child relationship. A super-club is the parent. A sub-club is the child that joins under it.

For example: imagine a city-wide NHS organization (the super-club) with individual school chapters underneath it (the sub-clubs). Each chapter logs their own hours, but the city-wide organization can see how all the chapters are contributing together.

It's a way to group clubs together and roll up their service hours into one bigger picture.

What's on the
Affiliates page?

🏆

Affiliation Leaderboard

If your club is part of a federation (a super-club), this shows how many approved hours each sub-club has contributed to the parent. Only visible when your club is affiliated.

🔍

Find Clubs to Follow

Search for any club and "bookmark" it. This is separate from affiliation — it's just a way to follow clubs you're interested in and see their events.

📅

Bookmarked Club Events

Events from clubs you've bookmarked appear here in a live feed. Great for staying informed about what other clubs are doing, even if you're not a member.

💡 Affiliation vs. bookmarking
Bookmarking a club is casual — you just follow their events. Affiliation is an official connection between clubs where hours flow between them. They're different things.

⚙️
Affiliated clubs only

What is the
System Log?

Once your club is officially affiliated as a sub-club under a super-club, a special log called the System Log appears automatically in your Log Hours page.

This log is special: when you submit hours to the System Log and they get approved, those hours don't just count for your club — they also flow up to your super-club's Affiliates leaderboard.

It's how sub-club contributions get tracked at the parent level.

The flow of hours

📝You submit hours to the System Log
↓
✅Your club admin approves them
↓
🏆Appear on the super-club's Affiliates leaderboard
ℹ️ You can't submit directly
Sub-clubs submit hours via the System Log in their own club. You cannot submit directly into the super-club's logs.

Sidebar → History

Viewing your
submission history

The History page shows all your archived submissions — hours you submitted in past school years that have been closed out by your admin. It's your permanent record.

Submissions are organized by club, then by period. You can see your stats (total hours, number of submissions) and scroll through each individual entry.

📅 When does it populate?
History only fills up after your admin archives a school year. If you're new, it will be empty until then — that's normal.
