My Club → Club Settings / Admin
Club-level affiliation

Become an
affiliated sub-club

A club admin can request to affiliate their club under a parent super-club. Once approved, your club's hours feed into the parent's affiliation leaderboard.

1
Admin opens Club Settings
Go to My Club → Settings tab (visible only to admins). Find the Affiliation section.
2
Search for the parent club
Type the super-club name, select it, and send the affiliation request.
3
Wait for approval
The parent club's admin must approve the request. Once approved, your hours automatically federate upward via the System Log.
🔗 System Log
When affiliated, a System Log appears in your Hours Logs. Sub-clubs submit hours here; they flow to the parent club automatically.

Sidebar → Log Hours → System Log
After affiliation is approved

Managing
affiliation hours

Once your club is an approved sub-club, a System Log appears in Log Hours. This is where you submit hours that count toward the parent super-club's totals.

1
Select the System Log
It appears in your log period dropdown with a special "System" badge. You submit hours to it just like any other log.
2
Submit hours as usual
Same form — activity, date, hours, description. These go to your club's admin for approval.
3
Approved hours federate up
Once approved, they appear in the parent club's Affiliates leaderboard under your club's name automatically.

Organizing submissions

Create & manage
log periods

Logs are named tracking periods (e.g., "Fall Semester") that group hour submissions. Volunteers pick a log when submitting. Admins open or close logs to control access.

1
Click "Add Log"
Enter a Log Name and optional Hours Required (minimum hours for the period). Click "Create Log."
2
Toggle Open / Closed
Use the switch on each log card to open or close it. Closed logs are read-only for volunteers.
3
Edit or delete
Use the pencil icon to rename or change the hours requirement; the trash icon to delete (after confirmation).

Admin Panel → Events
Running events

Create events &
QR check-in

Admins create events that volunteers can check in and out of using a QR code. Hours are calculated automatically from check-in/out timestamps.

1
Create an event
Set the event name, log period, location, date, description, and max attendance. Choose open, password-protected, or QR-only access.
2
Share the QR code
The event generates a QR code. Display it at the venue — volunteers scan in when they arrive and scan out when they leave.
3
Hours are auto-calculated
Check-in/out times calculate hours per volunteer. Optionally set a conditional hour grant if they exceed a threshold.

Admin Panel → History
Year-end admin task

Archive & view
submission history

At the end of a school year, admins archive all submissions into a historical snapshot. Volunteers can then view their own history under the History tab in the volunteer interface.

1
Admin view: "Archived Submissions"
Browse historical snapshots for any past period. Tabs for each archived school year; data shows student names, hours, activities.
2
Volunteer view: "My History"
Volunteers see their own archived submissions, organized by school year and log period. Empty until the first archive.
3
Export records
Archived data can be exported for reporting, college applications, or record-keeping outside the app.

Super-club admin task

Approving
affiliation requests

When another club sends an affiliation request to join your club as a sub-club, you'll see it in your Admin Settings under Affiliations. You decide whether to approve or deny.

1

Incoming requests appear here with the requesting club's name, description, and current hours.
2

3
Monitor on the Affiliates leaderboard
Once active, their approved System Log hours appear on the Affiliates page grouped by club name.
